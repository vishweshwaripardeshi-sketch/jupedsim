## My arithmetic (not in the book)
30.5 M / 3,700 m = ~8,200 people per metre of shoreline on the peak day; / 14 h = ~590 per metre per hour. Crude: assumes everyone uses the whole shoreline equally.
The headcount is disputed in the book itself (40-60 M vs 120 M reported over the festival), and 30.5 M is the book's estimated peak-day attendance.

## Other features noted, no bottleneck claimed by the book
Main street (gatta) raised ~24 in with 1x1 m steel plates; 14 sectors (sector 1 pre-built outside the floodplain, sector 4 near the Sangam); 85 CCTV at key junctions, 56 watchtowers, 12,500 police; flooding in sectors 7-8; 200,000 people lost or abandoned.

## Still missing for simulation
No scaled plan: the maps (e.g. p.53) have no scale bar, so no junction widths, bridge lengths or ghat access widths can be measured.
